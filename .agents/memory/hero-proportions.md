---
name: Hero proportions
description: Responsive hero artwork and contact area tradeoff for short mobile screens
---

Preserve the hero portrait's original proportions and place the contact controls in their own dark area. On short devices, allow the hero to extend below one viewport rather than stretch or crop the artwork or overlap the person with buttons.

**Why:** Forcing the entire artwork to the device's viewport height distorted the portrait and displaced controls from the dark section on a real phone.

**How to apply:** When adjusting hero sizing or mobile breakpoints, keep artwork aspect ratio independent of viewport height and ensure the controls stay below the portrait on a dark background.
