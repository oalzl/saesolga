---
name: News card motion
description: Intended visual behavior of the news carousel
---

Move the two news cards together as one page, with a visible gap between pages during transitions. Preserve the impression that the cards and their borders travel across the screen, not just the images and text inside fixed card frames.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose two cards moving together. Without a visible separation between adjacent pages, moving cards can look like stationary frames whose contents change.

**How to apply:** When changing news pagination or animation, keep the pair grouped and include the page gap in the slide distance, including the seamless last-to-first transition.
